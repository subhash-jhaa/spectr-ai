# Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

## Is there a free tier?
Yes, Spectr provides a free starter tier for individual projects, personal sites, and small websites with essential analytics and Web Vitals tracking.

## How do I add Spectr to my site?
Copy your site tracking script snippet from your project settings and paste it into the `<head>` tag of your website HTML or web application layout.

## What frameworks and browsers are supported?
Spectr works with any web framework including Next.js, React, Vue, Svelte, Nuxt, Astro, Angular, and static HTML. It supports all modern desktop and mobile browsers including Chrome, Safari, Firefox, Edge, and mobile WebKit.

## Does Spectr require a cookie banner?
No. Because Spectr uses no tracking cookies and does not collect personal identifying information (PII), cookie consent banners are not required.
